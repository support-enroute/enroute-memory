THE ART OF MAKING A MATCH

Your next connection
is one flip away.

  1. Flip two cards per move.
  2. Matching cards stay face up. If they do not match, they flip back after a moment.
  3. Find every pair to complete the board.

Scoring: +100 points per pair. Each consecutive match adds a further +25 bonus, up to +100 extra per pair. A mismatch costs 10 points (never below zero) and resets your streak. Time is tracked for reference.
